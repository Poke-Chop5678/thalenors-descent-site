About the Developer

A short note on who is building Thalenor's Descent.

Solo descent

Thalenor's Descent — Tournament of Heroes is an indie dark-fantasy board game built in Godot by an independent developer. The goal is a party-driven descent that feels like a living tabletop: Prep, movement, card clashes, and a Void that pushes back.

Development and Steam playtesting are ongoing. Feedback from playtesters shapes balance, onboarding, and multiplayer lobbies.

The game in brief

Four realms. One Void. Malakor's tournament summons a hero from each realm to face devilish trials. Lead the company, keep the hand full, unlock the Boss Gate, and bring Thalenor home.
