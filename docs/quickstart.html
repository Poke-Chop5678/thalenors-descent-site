Quickstart

Get from first launch to a solid first tournament. For edge cases, see the Rulebook.

Download PLAYER_GUIDE.txt

For full rules and edge cases, see PLAYERS_RULEBOOK.txt. This guide gets you from first launch to a solid first run.

QUICKSTART — Get Playing in Minutes

  1. START A RUN — From the main menu: Create New Party (or open Saved Parties), then Enter the Void. That opens the Void Lobby — the hub for every run. In Void Lobby set Play Style (Explore or Defend), world size, difficulty, and Game Rules, then Start New Run, Continue Run, or Multiplayer. How to Play (on the world-size path) opens a short sandbox tutorial with no save slot. Before a new run, Choose Your Heroes: pick avatar, class, and name for each realm (or Generate Random).
  2. TWO PLAY STYLES (EQUAL)
    • Explore: a living labyrinth. Prep, move, clash, clear Spawners and side quests, unlock the Boss Gate, defeat Malakor, and clear living enemies.
    • Defend: a wave arena around a center vortex. Clear each wave’s enemies before they reach the vortex (leaks cost party lives). Claim Wave Complete rewards and Journey Points, then push deeper into Abyssal waves.
    • Both use the same party, Prep, battles, and full hand of four. Pick either in Void Lobby; neither is a separate mode of the game.
  3. EACH ROUND HAS THREE THINGS YOU CONTROL (both play styles) Shared loop for Explore and Defend:

A. PREP (Selection)

  • For each highlighted hero:
    1. Optionally place Holding cards into slots (or discard if allowed). Battlescars and Cursed cards cannot be discarded.
    2. Choose an action:
      • BATTLE: Pick a gold-highlighted destination (click or drag the hero). They fight if enemies share that room after moves.
      • REST: Stay put (only on allowed floors; see Rulebook).
      • SEARCH: Dig your home-realm search square when available.
      • On Shop / Forge floors: open Shop or Forge from the menu without replacing your Prep action.

B. HERO MOVEMENT

  • Battle heroes move. Landing on orbs, card pedestals, or sigils collects them. Crossing a void crack injures vitality until after the next battle; landing on the crack clears it.

C. BATTLES

  • Every room with heroes and enemies battles. Drag a battle card onto an enemy (or double-click). Each hero acts once per battle round, then enemies strike. Win = loot; if all heroes on that room fall, you lose party lives.
  • Watch attack arrows: cross directions raise your Attack; head-on directions lower yours. Crits and focus come from the cards you play.
  1. WIN AND LOSE
    • Party lives depend on difficulty and Play Style (shown in the top bar). Explore examples: Easy 7, Normal 5, Nightmare 3. Defend starts at 4 on every difficulty. Lose one when a hero dies in battle. Explore: also when too many enemies are on the board at round start (over the enemy cap, if overrun is on). Defend: also when an enemy reaches the center vortex (a leak).
    • Explore win: defeat Malakor (Boss Gate) and clear living enemies — unlock the gate with Spawner artifacts + side quests, then gather.
    • Defend: clear each wave for rewards and Journey Points; game over at 0 party lives. High Journey Points are tracked per saved party.
    • Game over at 0 party lives in either Play Style.

That’s enough to play. Read below when you want more confidence.

Understanding the Flow

VOID LOBBY

Create Party / Saved Parties → Enter the Void → Void Lobby. Choose Explore or Defend, world size (Explore: How to Play through Colossal; Defend: How to Play + Small), difficulty, and Game Rules. Start New Run, Continue Run, or Multiplayer. Classes and kits follow the class you pick in Choose Your Heroes (Crusader, Reaper, Strider, Occultist) — not the realm corner alone.

ROUND LOOP (shared)

Invasion (round start)

→ Titles; defeated heroes respawn; enemies move; new spawns; pickups/hazards expire or appear (from round 2+).

Defend: enemies path toward the vortex; Scavengers may steal search loot on the way.

Prep

→ Place Holding cards if you want, then Battle / Rest / Search.

Hero movement

→ Battle heroes move; collect pickups; floor hazards apply.

Battles

→ One room at a time until cleared or your heroes fall.

After each battle

→ Summary, then loot for heroes who fought.

Cleanup

→ Next round begins with Invasion.

Defend: when the wave roster is clear, Wave Complete rewards play, then the board reshuffles for the next wave.

Plan in Prep: who fights where, who rests, who searches. Keep party lives up — Explore by clearing pressure; Defend by stopping vortex leaks.

Prep Phase — What to Do and Why

  • One hero at a time. Finish that hero before the next is selected. Prep order follows the four realms (Frostfall, Dustveil, Everglade, Shadowfen). Battle hands line up Frostfall, Shadowfen, Dustveil, Everglade.
  • Holding: new cards and shop buys wait here. Place into deck loadout, Stat Cards, modifiers, Extra, or discard (if allowed). The battle hand always shows 4 playable cards from your loadout — keep it full (replace only; never leave an empty battle slot). Shop → Sell sells the Holding top card for gems; you cannot sell from the battle loadout.
  • Battle vs Rest vs Search:
    • Battle: move and fight. Gold = reachable (range, voids, crystals, full rooms, and party tether all matter).
    • Rest: recover without fighting — only on allowed floors.
    • Search: dig your home search room for gems and finds (and fragments when not diverted to a Realm Rift).
  • Shop / Forge: stand on the floor, open the tab, spend gems / forge points. Does not replace your Prep choice.
  • Click a hero to focus the camera; click again to zoom out. In Prep, selecting a hero can enter move mode for a Battle destination.

Movement and the Board

  • Gold highlights = valid destinations.
  • Void cracks block range past them; you can land on the crack to clear it (and take void injury until after the next battle).
  • Crystals block a single square. Rooms hold at most 4 pawns.
  • Party tether: stay within the tether distance of every other hero (difficulty and Play Style change the room count). Yellow border lines show the tether edge.
  • Explore: Barrier Rooms seal maze branches. Finish every quest in the zone to receive that realm’s Barrier Rune, then move the matching-realm hero into the Barrier Room to clear it.
  • Defend: walls stay for the run; between waves, some rooms reshuffle while you regroup at the vortex.
  • Special floors matter: Thorns hurt as you step; Sprint boosts range; Sludge slows; Bugs locks focus; Quick Sand is deadly if you linger; teleports move the whole party when all four stand together.

Explore — Dungeons, Quests, and Malakor

  • Locked disciple / rift stairs need a realm key from a Hidden Chamber. Dig the chamber entrance’s last search square to reveal it; gather the party to enter. Matching-realm hero takes the key.
  • Disciple scrolls / Realm Rifts: unlock → clear floors → claim glyph or fragment at the Summon Room → Surface Staircase home (or seal the rift).
  • Clear Spawners to recover Spawner artifacts (top-bar tally). When all artifacts and side quests are done, gather at the Boss Gate for Malakor.
  • Watch the top-bar quest line inside side-quest dungeons.

Defend — Waves and the Vortex

  • Enemies spawn from open Spawners toward a wave quota, then a disciple pack arrives after a short countdown. Clear every living wave enemy (vortex leaks remove that enemy but cost a party life).
  • Wave Complete grants Journey Points, forge tokens, gems, and +1 party life, then the board reshuffles for the next wave.
  • Stop leaks: fight on the path to the vortex; do not let minions walk in.
  • Scavengers steal from search rooms before fleeing — clear them or lose finds.

Battles — How to Fight

  • Drag a battle card onto an enemy, or double-click to repeat / default to the strongest attacker. Each hero acts once per battle round.
  • Directional swings:
    • Arrow on your card = directional; no arrow = regular.
    • Enemy arrow = their next swing.
    • Cross (perpendicular) arrows: your attack gets +30%.
    • Head-on (opposite) arrows: your attack gets −30%.
    • Same direction or a regular side: base Attack (no swing change).
  • Dice: each side rolls 1–100. Roll ≥ (100 − Focus) = hit (Focus 70 → need 30+). Dice bonuses are +N on your roll. If both hit, both deal full attack damage. Hero crits (from the card) deal full damage and take none that clash.
  • Heal: restores the acting hero’s vitality with no clash. Block: you don’t roll to hit; you reduce incoming damage. At most one Heal and one Block in the hand of four.
  • Clear the room for loot. If all heroes there fall, you lose party lives and those heroes respawn next Invasion.
  • Captain+ enemies may use line specials (blink, darkness, stalking, etc.) or leave floor drops (ice, poison, spores, blight). Some board enemies appear as Unknown until you battle them. Disciples and Malakor can borrow unlocked specialties. Charged and Free Attacks can appear as you level; class specialty attacks unlock later in the Chronicle.

Staying Alive

  • Check the top bar for your starting party lives (difficulty + Play Style). Lose 1 on a battle defeat; Explore overrun and Defend vortex leaks also cost lives.
  • Vitality persists between rounds. Rest and Heal matter. Defeated heroes come back next Invasion with restored vitality.
  • Explore: clear crowded boards before the enemy count climbs over the cap.
  • Defend: prioritize threats near the vortex over distant side fights.
  • Use void cracks carefully: clear them when you must, but expect injury.
  • Explore: open Barrier Rooms early when a branch traps good floors (Shop / Forge / search). Watch Spawner artifacts and side quests — both unlock Malakor’s gate.

Rewards and Progression

  • After a won battle, choose loot → Holding → place it in Prep.
  • Orbs and sigils grant lasting or tournament-scoped bonuses. Card pedestals give a tier-1 card to Holding.
  • Keep a full deck loadout (starts at 5, grows toward 12) and a strong 4-card battle hand. Use Forge to upgrade cards and Shop to spend gems when you can reach them. Sell only from Holding top.
  • Saved parties keep Chronicle progress and Continue Run state. Many in-run bonuses reset when a new tournament / wave sequence begins; meta upgrades and unlocks can persist across runs.
  • Restricted Resting and other toggles live under Game Rules in Void Lobby (and difficulty presets), not a separate game mode.

Descent Chronicle (save meta)

  • Each party save has a Descent Chronicle: glyphs, cards, enemies, rooms, and four realm fragment puzzles. Nothing shows until you discover it.
  • Discover by playing: battle cards and enemies, enter rooms, collect glyphs and fragment pieces. Each discovery adds 100 to Descent Score.
  • Descent Score also sums your best Void Lobby scores for every size × difficulty option (Explore and Defend tracked where applicable). Putting a fragment in its correct puzzle slot adds another 100.
  • Fragments tab: pick a realm, then spend shared Puzzle moves (1 per swap) to unscramble the grid. Finding a piece grants +5 moves. When every piece is found but puzzles remain unsolved, each won game grants +5 more.
  • Open Chronicle from the party / Void Lobby menus (or in-game menu when shown).

Tips for the Whole Game

  • Plan in Prep so nobody is stranded alone against a packed room.
  • Prefer swing cards that give you the cross bonus — and be wary of head-on matchups that cut your Attack.
  • One action per hero per battle round: finishing a threat before it acts often saves more lives than a greedy trade.
  • Explore: finish zone quests for Barrier Runes, clear Spawners for artifacts, and finish side quests before pushing Malakor’s gate.
  • Defend: keep a hero near the vortex path; leaks hurt as much as a wipe.
  • Search Hidden Chambers for stair keys; clear Realm Rifts / glyph quests when safe — fragments and glyphs fuel Chronicle Rank.
  • When in doubt, open PLAYERS_RULEBOOK.txt for exact numbers and edge cases.
