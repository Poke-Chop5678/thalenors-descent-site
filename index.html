Tournament of Heroes

Malakor has torn a rift back from the Abyss and summoned the greatest hero of each realm. From the Void Lobby, choose your path: Explore a shifting labyrinth, or Defend a wave arena. Prep. Move. Clash. Survive the Void — or lose it.

Wishlist on Steam How to play

Two ways into the Void

One game. One hub. In Void Lobby pick Explore or Defend — equal Play Styles, same party of four, same full hand of battle cards.

  • Play Style

    Explore

    A living labyrinth that breathes each round — invasions, shops, forges, sealed branches, and trials. Prep your company, commit to Battle, Rest, or Search, then clash with a full hand of four. Unlock the Boss Gate, defeat Malakor, and bring the realms home.

  • Play Style

    Defend

    A dark wave arena around a center vortex. Spawners fill a quota; disciples follow. Stop enemies before they leak into the void — every leak costs a party life. Clear the wave for Journey Points, forge tokens, gems, and a life, then push into Abyssal depth.

Void Lobby

Create a party or open a saved one, then Enter the Void. Set world size, difficulty, and Game Rules. Start a new run, continue where you left off, or host multiplayer — friends join the same Void.

Before a new run, Choose Your Heroes: avatar, class, and name for each realm. Crusader, Reaper, Strider, or Occultist — kits and vitality follow the class you pick.

How you survive

  • Explore or Defend

    Labyrinth descent or wave arena — same Prep loop, same battles, different pressure.

  • Card battles, full hand

    Every hero keeps four battle cards — replace, never empty. Direction, clash, and the card you play decide the blow.

  • A party from four realms

    Frostfall, Shadowfen, Dustveil, Everglade. Spend Prep wisely and protect shared party lives. Zero lives ends the run.

  • Play together

    Steam lobbies for co-op. Host, invite friends, or share a join link — optional voice chat while you face the Void.

The Void

It began in the East, in the great kingdom of Thalenor. For an age the King and his advisor Malakor of Shadowfen kept the realms in harmony — until the traitor was unmasked and cast into the Void with his disciples.

Peace did not last. A rift opened in Shadowfen. Malakor returned, and issued a cruel decree: the greatest heroes would face his trials until the realms themselves broke.

Enter the Void Lobby. Explore the labyrinth or Defend the arena. Bring the realms home.
