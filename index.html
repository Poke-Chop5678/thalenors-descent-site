Descend into the Void

Thalenor's Descent is a party-based dungeon descent on a living board. Each round the maze breathes — invasions, shops, forges, sealed branches, and trials that break careless heroes.

Prep your company, commit to Battle, Rest, or Search, then clash with a full hand of four battle cards. Unlock the Boss Gate, defeat Malakor, and bring the realms home.

How you survive

  • A living dungeon

    Invasions reshape the board each round. Shops, forges, and sealed branches turn every tournament into a new maze.

  • Card battles, full hand

    Every hero keeps four battle cards — replace, never empty. Direction, clash, and the card you play decide the blow.

  • A party from four realms

    Build a company, spend Prep wisely, and protect shared party lives. Zero lives ends the run.

  • Play together

    Steam lobbies for co-op descent. Host, invite friends, or share a join link — then face the same Void.

Meet the company

Rogue, ranger, knight, and wizard — summoned to Malakor's tournament beneath a broken sky.

The Void

It began in the East, in the great kingdom of Thalenor. For an age the King and his advisor Malakor of Shadowfen kept the realms in harmony — until the traitor was unmasked and cast into the Void with his disciples.

Peace did not last. A rift opened in Shadowfen. Malakor returned, and issued a cruel decree: the greatest heroes would face his trials until the realms themselves broke.

Find the Boss Gate. Defeat Malakor. Bring the realms home.
