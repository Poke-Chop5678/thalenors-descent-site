The game

  • A living dungeon

    Each round, the board breathes: invasions, rooms, shops, forges, and sealed branches. Your party is tethered to the maze — not a corridor crawler.

  • Card battles, full hand

    Every hero holds four battle cards. Replace, don't empty. Direction, clash, and the card you play decide the blow.

  • A party from four realms

    Build a company, spend Prep on Battle, Rest, or Search, then commit to the board. Party lives are shared. Zero is the end.

  • Play together

    Steam lobbies for co-op descent. Host, invite, or share a join link — then face the same Void.

The Void

It began in the East, in the great kingdom of Thalenor. For an age the King and his advisor Malakor of Shadowfen kept the realms in harmony — until the traitor was unmasked and cast into the Void with his disciples.

Peace did not last. A rift opened in Shadowfen. Malakor returned, and issued a cruel decree: the greatest heroes would face his trials until the realms themselves broke.

Find the Boss Gate. Defeat Malakor. Bring the realms home.
