Player guide

How to Play

Enough to start a run with confidence. In-game, open How to Play from the start menu for the sandbox tutorial.

Win and lose

  • Win: defeat Malakor and clear living enemies. Spawner artifacts plus side quests unlock the Boss Gate.
  • Lose: party lives reach 0 (you start with 5).
  • Overrun: too many living enemies on the board at round start costs 1 party life — not instant game over.

The round

  1. Invasion — titles, enemy movement, new spawns, hazards.
  2. Prep — place Incoming cards if you want, then choose Battle, Rest, or Search (Shop/Forge from those floors without replacing your Prep action).
  3. Hero movement — Battle heroes move; collect orbs, pedestals, and sigils.
  4. Battles — every room with heroes and enemies clashes.
  5. Loot — then the next round begins.

Prep actions

  • Battle: pick a gold-highlighted destination. Fight if enemies share that room after moves.
  • Rest: stay put on allowed floors (Home, Camp, Shop, Forge, search rooms, stairs, sigil rooms). Home Rest recovers more Vitality.
  • Search: dig your home-realm search square when available.

Battles

  • Every hero has a full hand of 4 battle cards. You replace cards — you never leave empty hand slots.
  • Drag a battle card onto an enemy (or double-click). Each hero acts once per battle round, then enemies strike.
  • Watch attack arrows: opposite directions buff the enemy; perpendicular directions buff you.
  • Mutual hit = full damage each. Win the room for loot; if all heroes in the room fall, you lose party lives.

Board systems

  • Barrier Runes clear sealed branches.
  • Hidden Chambers hold stair keys.
  • Disciple / rift quests end at Summon Rooms.
  • Party tether: heroes must stay near each other — gold highlights only show legal destinations.
