How to Play

In-game, open How to Play from the start menu for the sandbox tutorial. Use the guides below for the full written Quickstart and Rulebook from the game docs.

  • Hints

    Short tips from the in-game tutorials — light on spoilers.

At a glance

  • Win: defeat Malakor and clear living enemies. Spawner artifacts plus side quests unlock the Boss Gate.
  • Lose: party lives reach 0 (you start with 5).
  • Overrun: too many living enemies at round start costs 1 party life — not instant game over.
  • Round: Invasion → Prep → Hero movement → Battles → Loot → next round.
  • Battle hand: exactly 4 cards — replace only; never leave empty slots.
