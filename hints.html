Guidance

Hints

Short tips drawn from the in-game tutorials. Light on spoilers — enough to stop common early mistakes.

Four stats that matter early

  • Vitality — a hero's health.
  • Range — how far that hero can move each turn.
  • Focus — how reliably they land hits in battle.
  • Power Points — the budget that shapes card strength.

Board & party

  • Hover board elements for info before you commit a move.
  • Keep the party tether in mind — gold move highlights only show legal destinations.
  • Rest on Home for double Vitality recovery when you can spare the turn.
  • Double-click an enemy pawn to inspect its profile and deck before a fight.

Battle

  • Your 4-card hand is what you fight with. Block and Heal matter — not only Attack.
  • Heroes in a fight may act in any order; each acts once per battle round.
  • When you attack, expect a return strike. Damages resolve independently.
  • If multiple enemies are present, click a pawn to choose your target.

Shop, Forge & Deck

  • Shopping and forging do not spend your Prep action — buy or upgrade, then finish Prep.
  • Gems buy cards at the Shop; forge tokens upgrade card stats at the Forge.
  • Incoming and Queue hold new cards — place them carefully. The battle hand must stay full (exactly 4).
  • Outside battle you can rearrange freely; never leave an empty hand slot.

Pressure

  • Clear enemy pressure before the board cap starts eating party lives.
  • Plan in Prep: who fights where, who rests, who searches.
  • You can turn Game Tips off in Settings anytime — and reopen tutorials from the menu.
